Kamakura, an hour south of Tokyo, was Japan's medieval capital — today it's temples, hiking trails, and a surf beach. If you come a day early for the wedding, don't miss:

- **The Great Buddha (Daibutsu)** — 13 meters of serene bronze, sitting outdoors since 1252
- **Hokokuji** — a small temple with a bamboo grove and a tea house inside it
- **Komachi-dori** — the snack street; try the purple sweet-potato soft serve even in January
- **The Enoden line** — a tiny green train that runs along the sea to Enoshima
