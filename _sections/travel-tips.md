title: Japan Survival Tips
---
- **Cash still matters.** Cards work most places now, but keep ¥10,000 or so on hand. 7-Eleven ATMs take foreign cards.
- **Convenience stores are your friend.** Meals, coffee, heat packs, umbrellas, ATMs — all excellent, all open 24/7.
- **Trains stop around midnight.** Plan your night or budget for a taxi.
- **Trash cans are rare.** Carry a small bag; locals do.
- **Tipping isn't a thing.** Really. It causes polite confusion.
- **Learn two words.** *Sumimasen* (excuse me / sorry / thank you) and *arigatou* (thank you) will carry you far.
- **eSIM before you fly.** Ubigi or Airalo data plans are cheap and activate on landing.
