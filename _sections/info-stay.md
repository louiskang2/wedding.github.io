Most guests are staying in **Tokyo**, where the shuttle departs. A few options at different budgets (we have no affiliation — just places we like):

| Hotel | Area | Approx./night |
|---|---|---|
| Hotel Yaenoki *(sample)* | Shinagawa | ¥14,000 |
| The Canvas Ginza *(sample)* | Ginza | ¥22,000 |
| Sakura Hostel *(sample)* | Asakusa | ¥5,000 |

If you'd rather wake up by the sea, Kamakura and neighboring Enoshima have lovely small inns — book early, January weekends fill up.
