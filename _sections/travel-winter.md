title: Winter in Japan
---
January is one of Japan’s best-kept secrets: crisp blue-sky days in Tokyo, snow monkeys in the mountains, and the year's best powder snow up north. Crowds are thin and the light is beautiful.

Tokyo winters are mild (rarely below 0°C) but the mountains are serious snow country — pack for both if you're joining the snow trip.
