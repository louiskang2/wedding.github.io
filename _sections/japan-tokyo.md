A few of our favorite things, roughly north to south:

- **Yanaka** — old-Tokyo lanes, temple cats, and the best taiyaki we know
- **Meiji Shrine at opening hour** — forest silence in the middle of the city
- **Shibuya & Shimokitazawa** — record shops, vintage stores, natural wine
- **TeamLab Planets** — barefoot digital art; book tickets ahead
- **Tsukiji Outer Market** — go hungry, leave happy
