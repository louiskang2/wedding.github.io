title: Skiing in Yuzawa

Yuzawa has a dozen resorts within a short bus ride. Our plan:

- **Day 1 — GALA Yuzawa:** the gondola leaves directly from the shinkansen station. Full rental (skis/board, boots, wear) available on site — no gear needed.
- **Day 2 — Kagura:** bigger mountain, lighter powder, longer runs for the confident.

Lift tickets run about ¥6,000–8,000/day and full rentals about ¥10,000/day. Never skied? Yuzawa's beginner slopes are gentle, and English lessons can be booked — tell us in your RSVP comments and we'll arrange it.
