The weekend has three parts — come to as many as you can:

1. **The wedding** (Sat, Jan 16 · Kamakura) — ceremony and dinner reception. The main event; we'd love everyone there.
2. **The afterparty** (Sat, Jan 16 · Tokyo) — optional, adults-only, very optional shoes.
3. **The snow trip** (Mon–Wed, Jan 18–20 · Echigo-Yuzawa) — an optional two-night ski and onsen escape. Details on the [Snow Trip page]({{ '/snow-trip/' | relative_url }}).

Children are warmly welcome at the ceremony and reception. A quiet room with toys and a babysitter will be available at the venue.
