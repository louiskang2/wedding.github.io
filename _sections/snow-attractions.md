Not skiing? Yuzawa still earns the trip:

- **Onsen everywhere** — our lodge has indoor and outdoor baths; sitting in hot spring water while snow falls on your head is the whole point of January
- **Ponshukan** at the station — a sake-tasting hall with 100+ local brews by the coin
- **Snow-country cuisine** — *hegi* soba, miso-grilled mochi, and Niigata's famous koshihikari rice
- **The Takahan ryokan** — where Kawabata wrote *Snow Country*; the lobby museum is free
