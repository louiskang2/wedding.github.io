---
**Dress code: winter formal.** Suits, dresses, kimono — anything you feel wonderful in. January in Kamakura hovers around 3–10°C (39–50°F), and part of the ceremony is outdoors under a heated canopy, so bring a warm coat and consider layers.

A short packing list:

- A warm coat you don't mind appearing in photos
- Comfortable shoes for garden gravel (save the heels for dinner)
- Handheld heat packs (*kairo*) — sold at every convenience store
- Your appetite
